[ SEC.01 / THE BASKET ]

What this is.

Dr. Jessica Brown opened Empowered Life Chiropractic in Palm Harbor in September 2026. For the grand opening, 18 local businesses each put something into one raffle basket: a service, a product, a gift card, a credit. One person wins the basket. Everyone else gets this list, so the businesses that showed up for a neighbor's opening get seen for it.

If you scanned the sign next to the basket, this is the page it pointed to. Tap a linked business to go straight to them.

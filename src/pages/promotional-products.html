[ SEC.07 / QUESTIONS ]

Asked and answered.

"Can you put my logo on something I already picked?"

Usually. Send the link or the exact item and how many. I'll tell you if I can source it, or find the closest trade equivalent and show you the difference.

"Do I need vector artwork?"

Not to get a quote. Send the best file you have; I'll tell you whether it's production-ready before anything gets ordered. Checking and setting it up for the imprint is part of the quote. Redrawing a low-res logo or original design is real design work, scoped and priced separately before I do it.

"What's the minimum quantity?"

Depends on the product. Some things work in a run of 25; pens usually start around 100; some items are priced by the case. Tell me the number you actually need and I'll tell you where the price breaks are.

"How long does it take?"

Most orders land in two to three weeks from proof approval; some products are faster, embroidery is slower. Tell me the date first. I'd rather rule an item out than sell you something that shows up after the event.

"Why not just order it online myself?"

You can, and if you know the exact item, imprint method and quantity, you should. What you're paying me for is the part before that: picking the right thing, catching the artwork problem, and reading the fee table so the cheap pen doesn't turn into the expensive order.

"Do you only serve Palm Harbor?"

No. I'm based in Palm Harbor and work with businesses across Tampa Bay. Local orders I'll often hand-deliver; anything else ships wherever it needs to go.

"Who owns the artwork?"

You do. Any production-ready artwork I create for your business is yours to keep and reuse.

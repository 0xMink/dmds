[ SEC.07 / OBJECTIONS ]

Asked and answered.

"I paid an SEO company before and got nothing."

Fair enough: this trade is full of retainer-and-vanish. The difference you can hold me to: I report calls and leads that came through the site, on the GROWTH plan, with call tracking that shows it line by line. If the report can't tie the work to the phone ringing, you should fire me too.

"A guy already did my website."

How's it performing? Search your trade and your town right now. Where do you show up? If the answer's "nowhere," the site exists but isn't working. That's the fixable kind of problem.

"What does it cost?"

The build band and both monthly tiers are published right above, along with what moves you inside the band. No email gate, no "request our pricing guide."

"What's the catch with the free diagnosis?"

There isn't one. You tell me what's broken; I look at your site and the searches around your trade; you get what I'd build, what it'd cost, and whether it's worth it. Sometimes the honest answer is "your site is fine" or "don't hire me." I do them because enough turn into work. If yours doesn't, the diagnosis is still yours to keep.

"How fast will Google send me jobs?"

Listing, tracking, and site improvements: weeks. Competitive rankings: months. Anyone who quotes you a date is guessing; what I'll show you instead is the monthly evidence of movement.

"We're too busy already."

Busy this month isn't booked year-round. The site is the backstop for the quiet months. And if you're permanently past capacity, you don't need volume, you need selection: build the site around the work you want and price the rest out. Good techs also check out companies before calling back about a job; a dead site costs you hires, not just leads.

"All my work is referral."

Referrals are the best work. The site's job is the month they dry up, and the homeowner a referral told about you who Googled you before calling and found nothing.

"Who owns it?"

You do. The site, the domain, the content: if we part ways, it all stays yours. No hostage hosting.

"What happens after I say yes?"

One-page agreement same day, deposit to start, I'll need photos, logo, and access. The build is scoped in weeks, not quarters. You get the number before the deposit, and you'll hear from me on a set cadence. You won't have to chase me.
